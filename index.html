Who We Are

Rooted in service, gathering, and joy.

A welcoming community that fosters understanding and appreciation of Sikhism at Penn State, open to all students.

We run on three ideas: seva (service), sangat (community), and chardi kala (rising spirit). Since 2006, that's meant monthly meetings, langars, and celebration.

ਸੇਵਾ Seva Service. Langar is always free.
ਸੰਗਤ Sangat Community. You belong here.

Transparency

Every dollar, on the record.

We handle gifts the way we handle langar: carefully, and in the open.

  • ✓Official account

    Gifts go to SPSA's account under Penn State's Associated Student Activities (ASA), never a personal account.

  • ✓Receipted

    Every gift is logged and acknowledged. You get a record.

  • ✓Spent on programming

    Funds serve SPSA members and the campus, nothing else.

  • ✓Reported

    Our finances follow Penn State rules and are reported to Student Activities.
